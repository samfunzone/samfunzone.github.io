Tamil row marker editor

Each pink ◆ marker is a boundary shared by two sounds. Click a yellow label to play just that slice — a white cursor tracks playback, and you can drag that cursor to scrub back and forth or use ⏸ Pause to stop mid-slice. Drag markers until every slice matches its label (first marker = start of the first sound, last marker = end of the last sound). Positions save in your browser automatically. When all rows are right, press ⬇ Download marker times and save row-markers.json into the scripts folder.

If a row has extra noise or a stray retake between two sounds, check that row's 🔧 Independent start/end box — its markers split into a separate ▶ start and ◀ end per sound so you can drag around the junk instead of splitting it down the middle.
